--- div 146-152
<div class="markdown-alert markdown-alert-note markdown-alert-kind-todo">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Mitigations</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Launch day API load:</strong> cache the latest-readings endpoint for 60 seconds and finish the load test before Monday.</li>
<li><strong>Sensor drift:</strong> check the pH sensor at Kittiwake Shoal before December 1, and compare every pH sensor with a water sample on each visit.</li>
<li><strong>Gateway outage:</strong> buoys keep unsent readings and resend them when the link returns; a spare gateway waits at Port Avery.</li>
<li><strong>Storm delay:</strong> winter maintenance carries a five-day weather buffer.</li>
<li><strong>Biofouling:</strong> copper mesh on the sensor cages, cleaned on every visit.</li>
<li><strong>Volunteer shortage:</strong> two backups for every launch-day role.</li>
</ul>
</div>

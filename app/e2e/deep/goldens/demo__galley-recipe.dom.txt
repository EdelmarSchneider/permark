        h2 id="method"
          a.anchor href="#method" aria-label="Link to Method"
            "#"
          "Method"
        ol
          li
            strong
              "Marinate the fish."
            ol
              li
                "Cut it into thick steaks or chunks about 5 cm across."
              li
                "Rub with the lime juice, crushed garlic and a little salt."
              li
                "Leave it in the cool box for 20 to 30 minutes while you slice everything else."
          li
            strong
              "Slice the vegetables."
            ol
              li
                "Onions and peppers in thin rings, tomatoes in rounds."
              li
                "Chop the cilantro, stems and all, and keep a handful of leaves for the end."
          li
            strong
              "Build the layers"
            "in a wide, heavy pot, off the heat."
            ol
              li
                "A splash of olive oil, then half the onions, tomatoes and peppers."
              li
                "The fish in a single layer, with its marinade."
              li
                "The rest of the vegetables, half the cilantro and the chili."
          li
            strong
              "Pour in the coconut milk"
            "and drizzle the dendê over the top."
          li
            strong
              "Cook covered"
            "over medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, until the fish flakes."
            ol
              li
                "Do not stir. Shake the pot gently now and then so nothing sticks."
              li
                "At sea, clamp the pot in the stove's fiddle rails before you light the burner."
          li
            strong
              "Finish."
            "Taste the broth, adjust the salt and lime, scatter the last of the cilantro, and let it rest off the heat for 5 minutes."
        p
          "Serve with white rice and farofa, cassava flour toasted in butter."
